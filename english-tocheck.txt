scheme.pmkisan.name = PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi
scheme.pmkisan.benefit = ₹6,000 every year, in three instalments of ₹2,000, straight into your bank account.
scheme.pmfby.name = PM Fasal Bima Yojana (crop insurance)
scheme.pmfby.benefit = Insurance money if your crop is damaged. The farmer pays only a small share: at most 2% in Kharif, 1.5% for Rabi food and oilseed crops, and 5% for commercial or horticulture crops (like potato).
scheme.kcc.name = Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
scheme.kcc.benefit = Farm loans up to ₹3 lakh at 7% interest. Only 4% if you repay on time. No security needed up to ₹2 lakh. Confirm with your bank.
scheme.shc.name = Soil Health Card
scheme.shc.benefit = A soil test for your field and a card that says which fertiliser to use and how much. A new test every 2 years.
